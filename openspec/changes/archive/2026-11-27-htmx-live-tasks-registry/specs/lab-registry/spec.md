## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Live substance list
`/lab/registry/` SHALL implement live list updates (web-app "Live lists"):

- Its filter form (`#filters`) updates `#substance-rows` in place with `morph:outerHTML` (each row carrying stable `id="substance-row-<pk>"`) and updates `#substance-count` out of band with `#substance-count:morph`, using `hx-get="/lab/registry/"`, `hx-push-url="true"`, `hx-sync="#main:replace"`, and scoped triggers (`submit`, `search from:#filter-q`, `input delay:200ms from:#filter-q`, `change target:select`) while keeping its `method="get"`, `action="/lab/registry/"`, and submit button for no-script use.
- When `group` or `class` holds a non-empty key that names no vocabulary term, the corresponding `<select>` SHALL keep that key as a selected `<option>` so subsequent live edits to `q` preserve the unknown filter until the user explicitly selects `All`.
- Its view returns the complete page for plain GET requests and HTMX history restores (`HX-Request: true` with `HX-History-Restore-Request: true`), varies on `HX-Request` and `HX-History-Restore-Request`, and sets `HX-Push-Url` on non-restore HTMX GET responses to `/lab/registry/` with trimmed non-empty `q`, `group`, and `class` parameters.

#### Scenario: Live filtering and canonical URL on the substance list
- **WHEN** a test client requests `/lab/registry/?q=+zimt+&group=+final-products+&class=`, `/lab/registry/?group=nope`, `/lab/registry/?group=nope&q=zimt`, `/lab/registry/?group=&q=zimt`, and `/lab/registry/?q=+` with `HX-Request: true`, and requests `/lab/registry/?q=zimt` once with both `HX-Request: true` and `HX-History-Restore-Request: true` and once without HTMX headers
- **THEN** all responses return complete page markup (`html`, `#main`, table, filter form) and vary on `HX-Request` and `HX-History-Restore-Request`; `#filters` carries `hx-get="/lab/registry/"`, `hx-push-url="true"`, `hx-sync="#main:replace"`, scoped triggers (`submit`, `search from:#filter-q`, `input delay:200ms from:#filter-q`, `change target:select`), `hx-target="#substance-rows"`, `hx-select="#substance-rows"`, `hx-swap="morph:outerHTML"`, and unique disjoint `hx-select-oob="#substance-count:morph"`; populated `#substance-rows` rows carry `id="substance-row-<pk>"`; `/lab/registry/?group=nope` and `/lab/registry/?group=nope&q=zimt` render an empty `#substance-rows`, `#substance-count` of `0`, and keep `nope` as the selected `<option>` in `select[name="group"]`, whereas `/lab/registry/?group=&q=zimt` lists matching substances; the five ordinary HTMX responses set `HX-Push-Url` to `/lab/registry/?q=zimt&group=final-products`, `/lab/registry/?group=nope`, `/lab/registry/?q=zimt&group=nope`, `/lab/registry/?q=zimt`, and `/lab/registry/`; and the history-restore and plain GET responses omit `HX-Push-Url`
