## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Demo collection
The sample procedures SHALL form the demo collection `examples/demo/` of the application, a complete collection as the `collection` capability defines it, with the structure images in `examples/demo/static/structures/`, the compound registry in `examples/demo/compounds/` and the sequences in `examples/demo/md/sequences/`. The registry holds a file for every compound a sample names, as product or substrate, and no other file, and every compound sets `name_de`. Every path that this capability names under `org/procedures/` or `md/procedures/` is relative to `examples/demo/`. The application's own root holds no `org/`, `md/` or `site/static/` directory.

The demo declares compound fields of every type (compound-fields "Field declarations"):
- **Declarations.** `examples/demo/orgsyndb.toml` declares, in this order, `item_number` (`text`, `required`, `unique`, `pattern = 'CH-\d{4}'`, `column`, `search`), `short_name` (`text`, `search`), `chemical_class` (`choice`, `required`, `multiple`, `column`, `facet`, `vocabulary = "chemical-classes"`), `stock` (`number`, `unit = "g"`, `min = 0`), `in_house` (`boolean`, `facet`) and `group` (`choice`, `required`, `facet`, `tabs`, `vocabulary = "inventory-groups"`), each with a `label` and a `label_de`; `group` is labelled `Group` and `Gruppe`.
- **Vocabularies.** `examples/demo/vocabularies/` holds exactly `chemical-classes.toml`, `inventory-groups.toml` and `projects.toml`, and every value of each has a `label_de`. Some value of `chemical-classes` has two ancestors. `inventory-groups` holds, in this order, `purchased` (`Purchased`, `Zugekauft`), `starting-materials` (`Starting materials`, `Ausgangsstoffe`) and `reagents` (`Reagents`, `Reagenzien`), both under `purchased`, `synthesised` (`Synthesised`, `Selbst hergestellt`), and `intermediates` (`Intermediates`, `Zwischenprodukte`) and `final-products` (`Final products`, `Endprodukte`), both under `synthesised`.
- **Values.** Every compound sets `item_number`, `chemical_class` and `group`, a `group` of `starting-materials`, `reagents`, `intermediates` or `final-products`, each of them set by some compound; ten compounds are purchased and ten synthesised. At least one compound sets two chemical classes, and at least one a class with two ancestors. Some compounds set `short_name` and `stock` and some do not, and at least one `stock` is not a whole number. `in_house` is `true` for at least one compound, `false` for at least one, and unset for at least one.

The demo also declares procedure fields of every type (procedure-fields "Procedure field declarations"):
- **Declarations.** After its compound fields, `examples/demo/orgsyndb.toml` declares, in this order, `notebook` (`text`, `unique`, `pattern = '[A-Z]{2}-\d+/\d+'`), `project` (`choice`, `facet`, `vocabulary = "projects"`), `working_time` (`number`, `unit = "h"`, `min = 0`) and `inert_atmosphere` (`boolean`, `facet`), labelled `Lab notebook` and `Laborjournal`, `Project` and `Projekt`, `Working time` and `Arbeitszeit`, and `Inert atmosphere` and `Schutzgas`. None of them is `required`.
- **Vocabulary.** `projects.toml` holds, in this order, `teaching` (`Teaching`, `Lehre`), `first-year-practical` (`First-year practical`, `Grundpraktikum`) and `advanced-practical` (`Advanced practical`, `Fortgeschrittenenpraktikum`), both under `teaching`, `research` (`Research`, `Forschung`), and `biaryls` (`Biaryls`, `Biaryle`) and `cinnamates` (`Cinnamates`, `Zimtsäurederivate`), both under `research`.
- **Values.** Every sample procedure, in either language and either format, the general procedure included, sets `notebook` and `project`, a `project` of `first-year-practical`, `advanced-practical`, `biaryls` or `cinnamates`, each of them set by some procedure; its language variants set the same values. Some procedures set `working_time` and some do not, and at least one `working_time` is not a whole number. `inert_atmosphere` is `true` for at least one procedure of a research project, `false` for at least one procedure, and unset for at least one. `OSDB-0002` sets `notebook = "AE-1/2"`, `project = "first-year-practical"`, `working_time = 2.5` and `inert_atmosphere = false`.

#### Scenario: Samples live in the demo collection
- **WHEN** a test inspects the application
- **THEN** `examples/demo/orgsyndb.toml` exists, `examples/demo/org/procedures/OSDB-0001-salicylic-acid.org` and `examples/demo/md/procedures/OSDB-0002-aspirin.md` exist, the demo registry's file names are exactly the keys the samples name, each with `.toml`, and neither `org/`, `md/` nor `site/static/` exists at the application root

#### Scenario: The demo declares its fields
- **WHEN** a test reads `examples/demo/orgsyndb.toml`, `examples/demo/vocabularies/`, the demo registry and the demo's sample procedures
- **THEN** the declarations, the vocabularies, the compounds' values and the procedures' values meet the rules above
