## Summary

Thiamine-catalysed condensation of benzaldehyde gives benzoin. This is sample data; the quantities are not experimentally verified.

## Reagents

| Reagent | CAS | MW (g/mol) | Amount | mmol | Equiv |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Benzaldehyde | 100-52-7 | 106.12 | 2.122 g | 20 | 1.0 |
| Sodium hydroxide | 1310-73-2 | 40.00 | 0.080 g | 2 | 0.1 |
| Thiamine hydrochloride | 67-03-8 | 337.26 | 0.337 g | cat. | cat. |
| Ethanol | 64-17-5 | 46.07 | 10 mL; plus some for recrystallisation | – | – |

## Procedure

1. Dissolve thiamine hydrochloride in 2 mL water in a flask.
2. Add ethanol and cool the solution in an ice bath.
3. Dissolve sodium hydroxide in 2 mL water and add it slowly with stirring.
4. Add benzaldehyde to the cold mixture.
5. Fit a condenser and warm the stirred mixture to about 60 °C for 90 minutes.
6. Cool to room temperature, then in ice to complete crystallisation.

## Workup and Purification

Collect the solid by suction filtration and wash with cold water. Recrystallise from aqueous ethanol, cool, filter and dry. Expected yield: 1.49 g (7.00 mmol, 70 %).

## Characterization

White to pale yellow crystals; expected IR bands near 3400 (O–H) and 1680 cm⁻¹ (C=O).

## Notes

1. Keep the catalyst mixture cold during base addition.
2. Allow the mixture to cool slowly before using ice.
3. Two molecules of benzaldehyde furnish one molecule of benzoin.

## Hazards and Waste Disposal

Benzaldehyde: harmful if swallowed; irritates skin; Sodium hydroxide: causes severe burns and eye damage; Thiamine hydrochloride: dust may irritate eyes and airways; Ethanol: highly flammable; eye irritant; Benzoin: may irritate skin and eyes. Work in a fume hood; wear gloves and eye protection. Collect the alcoholic filtrates as organic waste and aqueous washings as aqueous waste.
