## Procedure

1. Combine benzaldehyde, ethyl acetoacetate and urea in ethanol.
2. Add aqueous hydrochloric acid with stirring.
3. Fit a reflux condenser.
4. Heat at reflux for 2 hours.
5. Cool to room temperature.
6. Add water and cool in ice to complete precipitation.

## Workup and Purification

Collect the solid by suction filtration, wash with water and dry.

## Characterization

White solid; IR (ATR): carbonyl bands near 1700 and 1650 cm⁻¹. Expected ¹H NMR: aromatic signals at δ 7.2–7.4 and a ring CH near δ 5.2.

## Notes

1. The product can crystallise on cooling; allow time for precipitation before filtration.
2. Check the identity and purity of the product before further use.
