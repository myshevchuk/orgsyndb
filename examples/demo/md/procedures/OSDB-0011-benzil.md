## Summary

Benzoin is oxidised to benzil with ammonium nitrate and copper(II) acetate. This is sample data; the quantities are not experimentally verified.

## Reagents

| Reagent | CAS | MW (g/mol) | Amount | mmol | Equiv |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Benzoin | 119-53-9 | 212.25 | 2.122 g | 10 | 1.0 |
| Ammonium nitrate | 6484-52-2 | 80.04 | 2.001 g | 25 | 2.5 |
| Copper(II) acetate | 142-71-2 | 181.63 | 0.046 g | cat. | cat. |
| Acetic acid | 64-19-7 | 60.05 | 8 mL | – | – |

## Procedure

1. Place benzoin and ammonium nitrate in a flask.
2. Add acetic acid and 2 mL water, followed by copper(II) acetate.
3. Fit a reflux condenser and stir the mixture.
4. Heat at reflux for 90 minutes in a fume hood.
5. Allow the mixture to cool to room temperature.
6. Slowly add water with stirring and cool the mixture in ice.

## Workup and Purification

Collect the yellow solid by suction filtration, wash with cold water and dry in air. Expected yield: 1.47 g (7.00 mmol, 70 %).

## Characterization

Yellow crystals; expected IR absorption near 1660 cm⁻¹ for the conjugated diketone.

## Notes

1. The condenser must remain open to the atmosphere.
2. Cool before dilution to limit splashing.
3. Copper-containing filtrates require separate collection.

## Hazards and Waste Disposal

Benzoin: may irritate skin and eyes; Ammonium nitrate: oxidiser; may intensify fire; Copper(II) acetate: harmful if swallowed; toxic to aquatic life; Acetic acid: flammable and corrosive; Benzil: may irritate skin and eyes. Work in a fume hood; wear gloves and eye protection. Collect copper-containing aqueous filtrates separately as metal-containing waste.
