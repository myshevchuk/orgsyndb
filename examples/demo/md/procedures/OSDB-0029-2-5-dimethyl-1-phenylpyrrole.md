2,5-Dimethyl-1-phenylpyrrole. This is sample data; the quantities are not experimentally verified.

## Procedure

1. Mix hexane-2,5-dione and aniline in methanol.
2. Add aqueous hydrochloric acid with stirring.
3. Fit a reflux condenser.
4. Heat at reflux for 2 hours.
5. Cool to room temperature.
6. Add water and transfer to a separatory funnel.

## Workup and Purification

Extract with diethyl ether (25 mL total). Dry the combined organic layers over sodium sulfate, filter and evaporate to obtain the crude pyrrole as an oil. Recrystallise from aqueous methanol, filter and dry. Expected yield: 1.20 g (7.00 mmol, 70 %).

## Characterization

Colourless to pale crystals; literature melting point about 50–51 °C after recrystallisation from aqueous methanol; expected ¹H NMR: δ 7.1–7.5 (m, 5H), 5.9 (s, 2H), 2.1 (s, 6H).

## Notes

1. The acid promotes cyclocondensation; avoid contact with aniline throughout the workup.
2. Check the identity and purity of the product before further use.

## Hazards and Waste Disposal

Hexane-2,5-dione: harmful and a nervous system hazard; Aniline: toxic by skin contact, inhalation and ingestion; suspected carcinogen; Hydrochloric acid (1 M): may be corrosive to metals; causes skin and eye irritation; Methanol: highly flammable and toxic; causes organ damage; Diethyl ether: extremely flammable; forms explosive peroxides; Sodium sulfate: dust may irritate eyes; 2,5-Dimethyl-1-phenylpyrrole: dust or vapour may irritate. Work in a fume hood with gloves and eye protection. Collect aqueous and organic waste separately according to local rules.
