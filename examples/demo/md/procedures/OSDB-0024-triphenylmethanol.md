## Summary

Triphenylmethanol by Grignard addition. This is sample data; the quantities are not experimentally verified.

## Procedure

1. Dry the glassware and establish an inert atmosphere.
2. Place magnesium and a small crystal of iodine in the flask.
3. Add a little dry diethyl ether and bromobenzene to initiate the reaction.
4. Add the remaining bromobenzene in ether at a rate that maintains gentle reflux.
5. Stir until the magnesium has largely reacted.
6. Add methyl benzoate in ether slowly, then reflux for 1 hour.
7. Cool in an ice bath and cautiously add dilute sulfuric acid.

## Workup and Purification

Separate the organic layer, dry over sodium sulfate, filter and evaporate. Triturate the residue with petroleum ether, filter, and recrystallise the solid from 2-propanol.

## Characterization

White crystals; literature melting point approximately 160–163 °C. IR (ATR): broad O–H near 3400 cm⁻¹; ¹H NMR: aromatic multiplet near δ 7.2–7.5.

## Notes

1. Moisture destroys the Grignard reagent; control the ether reflux and quench residual reagent slowly.
2. Check the identity and purity of the product before further use.
