## Summary

(E)-Chalcone. This is sample data; the quantities are not experimentally verified.

| Reagent | CAS | MW (g/mol) | Amount | mmol | Equiv |

| Acetophenone | 98-86-2 | 120.15 | 1.202 g | 10.0 | 1.0 |

| Sodium hydroxide | 1310-73-2 | 40.00 | 0.080 g | cat. | cat. |
| Ethanol | 64-17-5 | 46.07 | 20 mL; plus some for washing | – | – |

## Procedure

1. Dissolve acetophenone and benzaldehyde in ethanol.
2. Dissolve sodium hydroxide in a little water.
3. Add the base solution to the stirred mixture.
4. Stir at room temperature for 1 hour.
5. Cool the mixture in an ice bath.
6. Allow the yellow product to crystallise.

## Workup and Purification

Collect the solid by suction filtration, wash with cold aqueous ethanol and dry.

## Characterization

Yellow crystals; literature melting point approximately 55–57 °C. IR (ATR): conjugated C=O near 1660 cm⁻¹; ¹H NMR: alkene doublets near δ 7.5–7.8, J about 16 Hz.

## Notes

1. The large alkene coupling in the NMR spectrum supports the E configuration.
2. Check the identity and purity of the product before further use.

## Hazards and Waste Disposal

Acetophenone: harmful if swallowed; eye irritant; Benzaldehyde: harmful if swallowed; irritant; Sodium hydroxide: causes severe burns; Ethanol: highly flammable; (E)-Chalcone: dust or vapour may irritate. Work in a fume hood with gloves and eye protection. Collect aqueous and organic waste separately according to local rules.
