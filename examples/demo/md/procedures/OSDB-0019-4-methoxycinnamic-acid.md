## Summary

4-Methoxybenzaldehyde and malonic acid give (E)-4-methoxycinnamic acid by condensation. This is sample data; the quantities are not experimentally verified.

## Reagents

| Reagent | CAS | MW (g/mol) | Amount | mmol | Equiv |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4-Methoxybenzaldehyde | 123-11-5 | 136.15 | 1.361 g | 10 | 1.0 |
| Malonic acid | 141-82-2 | 104.06 | 1.561 g | 15 | 1.5 |
| Piperidine | 110-89-4 | 85.15 | 0.10 mL | cat. | cat. |
| Pyridine | 110-86-1 | 79.10 | 10 mL | – | – |
| Hydrogen chloride | 7647-01-0 | 36.46 | 2 M, as needed | – | – |

## Procedure

1. Place 4-methoxybenzaldehyde and malonic acid in a flask.
2. Add pyridine and stir to form a mixture.
3. Add piperidine and fit a reflux condenser.
4. Heat the mixture at reflux for 90 minutes in a fume hood.
5. Cool to room temperature.
6. Pour slowly into 2 M hydrochloric acid with stirring and cool in ice.

## Workup and Purification

Collect the precipitated acid by suction filtration, wash with cold water and dry. Expected yield: 1.25 g (7.00 mmol, 70 %).

## Characterization

Pale solid; expected IR carboxylic acid C=O absorption near 1690 cm⁻¹ and trans-alkene ¹H NMR doublets near δ 6.3 and 7.7.

## Notes

1. The reaction releases carbon dioxide; do not seal the apparatus.
2. Add the mixture slowly to acid to control warming.
3. Check that the mixture remains acidic after mixing.

## Hazards and Waste Disposal

4-Methoxybenzaldehyde: harmful if swallowed; irritates skin; Malonic acid: harmful if swallowed; eye irritant; Piperidine: highly flammable, toxic and corrosive; Pyridine: highly flammable and harmful by inhalation; Hydrogen chloride: acidic solution irritates or burns skin and eyes; (E)-4-Methoxycinnamic acid: may irritate skin and eyes. Work in a fume hood; wear gloves and eye protection. Collect pyridine-containing acidic filtrates as labelled hazardous waste.
