## Summary

Acetanilide from aniline. This is sample data; the quantities are not experimentally verified.

## Reagents

| Reagent | CAS | MW (g/mol) | Amount | mmol | Equiv |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aniline | 62-53-3 | 93.13 | 0.931 g | 10.0 | 1.0 |
| Acetic anhydride | 108-24-7 | 102.09 | 1.225 g | 12.0 | 1.2 |
| Sodium acetate | 127-09-3 | 82.03 | 1.231 g | 15.0 | 1.5 |
| Hydrogen chloride | 7647-01-0 | 36.46 | 1 M, 10 mL | – | – |

## Procedure

1. Place aqueous hydrochloric acid in a flask.
2. Add aniline with stirring until dissolved.
3. Add sodium acetate in portions.
4. Add acetic anhydride slowly while stirring.
5. Stir for 30 minutes at room temperature.
6. Cool the mixture in an ice bath to complete crystallisation.

## Workup and Purification

Collect the acetanilide by suction filtration, wash with cold water and dry the solid.

## Characterization

White crystals; literature melting point approximately 113–115 °C. IR (ATR): amide C=O near 1660 cm⁻¹ and N–H near 3300 cm⁻¹.

## Notes

1. The acetate buffers the mixture and permits the amine to react with the anhydride.
2. Check the identity and purity of the product before further use.

## Hazards and Waste Disposal

Aniline: toxic by skin contact, inhalation and ingestion; suspected carcinogen; Acetic anhydride: corrosive and harmful; combustible; Sodium acetate: dust may irritate eyes; Hydrochloric acid (1 M): may be corrosive to metals; causes skin and eye irritation; Acetanilide: harmful if swallowed. Work in a fume hood with gloves and eye protection. Collect aqueous and organic waste separately according to local rules.
