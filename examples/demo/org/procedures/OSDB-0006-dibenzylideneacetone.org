* Summary
(1E,4E)-Dibenzylideneacetone (1,5-diphenylpenta-1,4-dien-3-one) by a
double Claisen–Schmidt condensation of benzaldehyde with acetone in
aqueous ethanolic sodium hydroxide. The yellow product precipitates from
the reaction mixture and is recrystallised from ethyl acetate. This is
sample data: the quantities follow the textbook method but are
not experimentally verified.

* Procedure
1. Dissolve the sodium hydroxide (2.00 g, 50.0 mmol) in water (20 mL) in
   a 100 mL conical flask, add ethanol (16 mL), and let the solution
   cool to 20 to 25 °C in a water bath (Note 1).
2. In a small beaker, mix the benzaldehyde (2.12 g, 2.03 mL, 20.0 mmol)
   with the acetone (0.581 g, 0.735 mL, 10.0 mmol).
3. Add half of this mixture to the vigorously stirred alkaline solution.
   A yellow cloudiness appears within a minute and a flocculent
   precipitate forms soon after.
4. After 15 minutes, add the rest of the mixture, rinsing the beaker
   with ethanol (1 mL), and stir for a further 30 minutes, keeping the
   bath at 20 to 25 °C (Note 2).
5. Collect the yellow solid by suction filtration and wash it on the
   filter with plenty of cold water until the washings are neutral to
   indicator paper (Note 3).

* Workup and Purification
Press the solid dry on the filter and dry it in air, then recrystallise
it from hot ethyl acetate, using about 2.5 mL of solvent per gram of
crude product. Cool the solution to room temperature and then in ice,
collect the crystals by suction filtration, wash them with a little
ice-cold ethanol and dry them in air. Expected yield 1.99 g (8.50 mmol,
85 %).

* Characterization
Pale yellow crystals. Determine the melting point. The IR spectrum (ATR)
shows the conjugated ketone C=O stretch near 1650 cm⁻¹. Expected ¹H NMR
signals (CDCl₃): δ 7.75 (d, J = 15.9 Hz, 2H), 7.62 (m, 4H), 7.42 (m,
6H), 7.10 (d, J = 15.9 Hz, 2H). The large coupling constant of the
vinylic doublets shows the (E,E) configuration.

* Notes
1. Keep the temperature below 30 °C. Warmer mixtures give a sticky
   product contaminated with resinous side products.
2. Adding the aldehyde and ketone in two portions limits the formation
   of benzalacetone, the product of a single condensation.
3. Residual sodium hydroxide makes the product darken on storage, so
   wash it thoroughly.

* Hazards and Waste Disposal
Sodium hydroxide solution causes severe burns to skin and eyes; wear
gloves and eye protection. Benzaldehyde is harmful if swallowed. Acetone,
ethanol and ethyl acetate are highly flammable; keep them away from
flames and hot plates. Dibenzylideneacetone may irritate skin and eyes.
Neutralise the alkaline filtrate and washings with dilute acid before
disposing of them as aqueous waste. Collect the recrystallisation mother
liquor as non-halogenated organic waste.
