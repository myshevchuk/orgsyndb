{{- /* The overall yield of a sequence as a whole percentage: the product of the yields of its steps (sequence-steps.html), rounded to the nearest whole number, halves up. Takes the slice of steps; returns an integer. The product is kept in percent, each step multiplying by its yield and dividing by 100, and rounded once: a product of whole-number yields that ends in .5 is then exact. */ -}} {{- $total := 100.0 -}} {{- range . }}{{ $total = div (mul $total (float .yield)) 100 }}{{ end -}} {{- return int (math.Round $total) -}}
