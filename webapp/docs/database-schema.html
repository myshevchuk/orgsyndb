Tables

accounts_accesstoken

A personal access token, by which a program outside the browser acts as the account user.

The token itself is never stored, only its SHA-256 digest, so it cannot be recovered from the row. Deleting the row revokes the token; deleting the account deletes its tokens. last_used_at is None until a request is made with the token.

accounts_user

A person who signs in or is named on records.

USERNAME is at most 40 ASCII letters and digits, with single ., - or _ between them, the user names Forgejo accepts, unique regardless of case, and none that Forgejo reserves (validate_unreserved). Procedure files name their authors by it, so the admin offers no way to change it once the account exists. INITIALS, one to five letters or digits, name the user in lists; they are stored in upper case and unique regardless of case. EMAIL may be empty and is otherwise unique regardless of case. An account without a usable password cannot sign in and can still be named on records.

Once a save of an account whose is_active, email or display name changed has committed, its Forgejo account is set in line with the account as stored (accounts.forge); forge_failed then tells whether that failed.

collection_entrysubstance

A substance of a scope entry in one ROLE, at its position among the entry's substances of that role, from 1; a product may have a yield_percent.

collection_import

One build the builder reported, accepted or refused.

An accepted import has the release the builder staged and the site's language, and no errors; a refused one has neither, and its errors one per line. base is the accepted import the build was made after, or None; pushed_by is the account that pushed the commit, or None when the builder named none or no account has the name it named.

collection_issuedid

A procedure or sequence ID the application issued, key, kept once: issued to the account user, or adopted, with no account, from a collection that had it already; issued_at is when.

collection_procedure

A procedure of the collection, by its ID, as the accepted import last_import created it or last changed it.

A general procedure has scope entries a step names by their position; a specific one has one entry. journal_entry is the lab notebook entry it was written from and project the project it belongs to, each None for none; no two procedures that are not withdrawn name one journal entry.

collection_procedureauthor

An account credited as an author of a procedure, at its position among the procedure's authors, from 1; each account is an author of a procedure once.

collection_scopeentry

An entry of a procedure's scope, identified within the procedure by its signature (collection.importing.signature); position is its place in the procedure's file, from 1.

collection_sequence

A synthesis sequence of the collection, by its ID, as the accepted import last_import created it or last changed it.

collection_step

A step of a sequence, at its position from 1, naming the scope entry it carries out.

demo_demomark

The mark the demo data set leaves in the database it fills.

A database holding a mark is a demo database: its records are invented, and its pages say so. SEEDED_ON is the day the data set was written as of.

inventory_article

An article: one substance of the registry as the lab delivers it, in one or more container sizes, its items.

The substance is the article's primary key, so a substance has at most one article. The article number, the one delivery notes carry, is None until the article is first delivered and fixed once it is set; it is unique among articles. The category is the article's and every size's. Its saves and deletions take the lock (orgsyndb_web.lock).

inventory_deliveryline

A line of a delivery note: COUNT containers of ITEM.

inventory_deliverynote

A delivery note (Lieferschein) and its lines.

A current note books one delivery movement per line; a historical note records deliveries made before they could be booked and changes no quantity. A historical note is reversed whole, by its REVERSED flag; a current note's lines are reversed through their movements. The date, the kind and the lines are fixed once the note exists.

inventory_enteredconsumption

The consumption of an item in a quarter as entered for the time before the application: COUNT containers in quarter QUARTER, 1 to 4, of YEAR.

inventory_item

An article in one container size, counted in whole containers.

The article, size and unit identify the item and are fixed once it exists. Its substance, name, article number and category are its article's. The quantity changes only by movements; the change history covers the other fields.

inventory_lot

A bulk lot of a substance, not yet aliquoted, made in the lab or bought.

The amount is kept in the base unit of the lot's unit, mg or µmol, and changes only by the lot's movements. A lot at zero is used up. A lot made in the lab may name the TASK_STEP that made it, never a bought one. The substance, source, unit and step are fixed once the lot exists. A lot belongs to every item of its substance's article.

inventory_lotmovement

A change of a lot's amount: AMOUNT in the lot's base unit, and AMOUNT_AFTER, the lot's amount once it was booked.

A withdrawal names the TASK_STEP the amount went into; no other kind names a step. The reversal of a withdrawal names it as REVERSES, and a withdrawal is reversed at most once; the reversal of a filling is named by the item's movement instead.

inventory_movement

A change of an item's quantity.

COUNT is the containers moved, or for an opening count or a correction the quantity entered; QUANTITY_AFTER is the item's quantity once the movement was booked. DATE is the day it took place, BOOKED_AT the time it was booked. A delivery names its DELIVERY_NOTE; a filling from a lot, and the reversal of one, name the lot's movement as BULK_MOVEMENT; a reversal names the movement it REVERSES, which is reversed at most once.

journal_entry

An entry of a notebook, by its NUMBER from 1, unique within the notebook; written <code>/<number>, such as AE-2/8.

journal_notebook

A paper notebook of the lab: its CODE, as written on its cover; USER, the account whose notebook it is; and a NOTE.

The registry snapshot lists the notebooks, so their saves and deletions take the lock (orgsyndb_web.lock).

planning_parameters

The planning parameters, one record kept with its history.

Without a stored record the field defaults apply; see current.

registry_substance

A substance of the registry: a compound, or a mixture or solution taken as one, which articles, lots and tasks refer to. Its saves and deletions take the lock (orgsyndb_web.lock).

registry_substanceclass

A chemical class of a substance: one row per substance and class. Its change history records each class given to or taken from a substance, and who did it.

tasks_counter

A named counter that only counts up.

tasks_task

The synthesis of a substance, from planning to its report and the QC list.

A task's number, T- and at least three digits, is assigned when it is first saved and never given again. Its state follows from its dates and its cancelled and failed flags; see state. A task that is ended and has not failed has a batch and a product lot; see has_product_lot and clean. Its steps are the TaskStep rows of steps, which tasks.steps gives it from its sequence, an imported sequence, from the sequence's step start_step on, or as plain steps; start_step is 1 for a task without a sequence. A task that follows a sequence makes the sequence's target; see clean.

tasks_taskstep

A step of a task, at its position from 1.

entry is the scope entry the step carries out, or None for a step not written down as one; planned_import is the accepted import that was the latest when the step was planned, or None; done_on is the day it was done, or None while it is not; and journal_entry is the lab notebook entry it is written down in, or None.

vocabulary_chemicalclass

A chemical class, such as the carboxylic acids, which a substance belongs to.

vocabulary_group

A group of the inventory, such as the final products, which a substance is filed in.

vocabulary_project

A project of the lab, such as a course it teaches.

vocabulary_reactiontype

A type of reaction, such as an esterification.
